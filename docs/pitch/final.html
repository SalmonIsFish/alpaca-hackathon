02 — For judges new to this

No interest. No ambiguity.
No gambling.

Islamic commercial law screens capital before it screens returns. Wealth has to come from real trade, risk-sharing, or productive effort — never from lending at interest, never from a contract too ambiguous to evaluate, never from a wager on chance. An agent that claims to honor this mandate has to prove all three, not describe them.

Riba — interest

Profit must come from real economic activity or shared risk, not from lending money at a fixed rate.

Rules out: bonds, margin financing, leveraged borrowing, interest-bearing holdings.

Gharar — uncertainty

What's being bought, its price, and the ability to deliver it must be knowable to both sides at the time of the contract.

Rules out: one-sided quotes, unpriced or undeliverable positions, terms too ambiguous to assess.

Maysir — gambling

Wealth must be earned through effort, trade, or shared enterprise risk — not a zero-sum wager decided by chance.

Rules out: naked speculation, positions with no capacity or intent to own the underlying.

Most autonomous trading agents ignore this mandate entirely. This one enforces all three — in code, on every single trade, not as a stated policy. What that looks like in practice ↓

10PM MYT is market open.

Problem

Human-approval = not autonomous. Old bot required typing EXECUTE PAPER at 10PM. Judging scores autonomy + robustness.

LLM hallucination risk: one invented strike = non-compliant trade with no recall.

Our answer

Fail-closed gates. LLM never decides compliance. Six pure functions — Shariah, Structure, Gharar, Maysir, Riba, Risk — cover all three prohibitions of Islamic commercial law; any REJECT blocks submit.

LLM only writes a 1-3 sentence rationale (always positive) — the red Gate block is the verdict.

09 — Three prohibitions, not one

Riba, gharar, maysir.
All enforced in code.

The objection, stated against ourselves

Options are named in the gharar and maysir literature as examples of what is prohibited, and AAOIFI generally prohibits conventional options outright. We say so in our own docs rather than routing around it.

The objections turn on two words: naked, and pure speculation. A fully cash-secured put on a screened business, struck below market, is a different act — and each narrowing condition is a gate, not a claim.

docs/shariah/position-cash-secured-puts.md — engineering rationale, not a fatwa.
