02 — Why this, why autonomous

10PM MYT is market open.
Ethics can’t be a prompt.

Problem

Human-approval = not autonomous. Old bot required typing EXECUTE PAPER at 10PM. Judging scores autonomy + robustness.
LLM hallucination risk: one invented strike = non-compliant trade with no recall.

Our answer

Fail-closed gates. LLM never decides compliance. Shariah + Structure + Risk are pure functions; any REJECT blocks submit. LLM only writes a 1-3 sentence rationale (always positive) — the red Gate block is the verdict.
